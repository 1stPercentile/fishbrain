# G0: Fish1 licence

## Verdict
VERDICT: UNSTATED. No licence is attached to the Fish1 data: not to gs://fish1-public, gs://fish1-release or the CAVE fish1 datastack. Nothing grants commercial use. The only licence anywhere near Fish1 is CC-BY-NC 4.0, and it covers the bioRxiv MANUSCRIPT: Crossref lists it with content-version "vor" for DOI 10.1101/2025.06.10.658982. It does not cover the GCS data, but readers will easily confuse the two. The release site's data_policy.html offers "open-access" for "the global neuroscience community" and "independent researchers" to "publish findings", and it requires a citation. That is research language, and it names no licence. It is not a commercial grant, so it cannot count as permissive. One third-party paper, ConnectomeBench2 (MIT, arXiv 2606.21116v1, 19 Jun 2026), states that Fish1 is "released under ... CC BY-NC 4.0" and cites fish1-release.storage.googleapis.com. That site carries no such text today, and it did not in the 2025-07-02 Wayback capture or at GitHub VCG/fish1-release HEAD. The paper's parallel H01 CC-BY claim is also absent from the H01 landing page. Most likely it copied the preprint licences onto the datasets. I count it as one published reading that cannot be traced to its cited source, not as evidence. GATE CONSEQUENCE (the project's G0 rule: a non-commercial or silent licence means research work continues and nothing commercial is released): G1 continues and any commercial release stays blocked. One thing settles it: written permission from the authors (the correspondence addresses are on p.1 of the preprint). The alternative is to accept the risk of reading the data as CC-BY-NC-like, which forbids commercial use. CAVE: the auth server holds exactly one terms record (id 1, "flywire"). The account used has view access to fish1_public and fish1_edit with missing_tos = [], so no terms acceptance is required of a fish1 user. I could NOT read the dataset-to-terms mapping: /auth/api/v1/dataset returned [], and the dataset and terms detail endpoints answered 403 "Requires admin privilege". The FlyWire ToS text (flywire.ai/tos.html) mentions fish or zebrafish 0 times. Its CC-BY-NC terms cover FlyWire's EM data (Zheng, Lauritzen et al. 2018) and edits made in FlyWire. The brain of record is the public, login-free bucket, so the product needs no CAVE path at all. REQUIRED ATTRIBUTION (data_policy.html, verbatim): "Any work utilizing this resource must cite the primary resource paper: Petkova, M. D., Januszewski, M., et al. (2025). A connectomic resource for neural cataloguing and circuit dissection of the larval zebrafish brain. bioRxiv." Link: https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.06.10.658982v1 (doi 10.1101/2025.06.10.658982). Suggested credit line, built from index.html wording: "Brain wiring: Fish1 (Petkova, Januszewski et al., 2025, bioRxiv 10.1101/2025.06.10.658982), released by the Lichtman and Engert laboratories at Harvard University with the Connectomics team at Google. Used without endorsement." The policy also asks you to "acknowledge significant proofreading contributions" by community members. That clause does not bite while we use the automated agglomeration seg_241003_agg241003; it applies only if we later pull CAVE-proofread state. Do not reproduce the paper's figures or text on a commercial site: that manuscript is explicitly CC-BY-NC 4.0. Not legal advice. Whether connectome data (facts, a database) is copyrightable at all varies by jurisdiction, and I did not assess it.
